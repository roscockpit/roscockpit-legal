Privacy Policy — RosCockpit

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Leggi in italiano

This Privacy Policy describes how RosCockpit ("the App", "we") collects, uses, and protects user information ("you", "the user"). Please read it carefully before using the App.

RosCockpit is a market analysis and intelligence tool designed for traders and investors. It does not provide financial, investment advice, or personalized recommendations. The information presented in the App is for informational and educational purposes only.

1. Who we are

RosCockpit is developed and operated by Gianni Rosella, based in Italy. For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the processing of your data, you can contact us at: info@roscockpit.com.

2. Age requirements

Use of RosCockpit is permitted exclusively to adults (18 years or older), regardless of the minimum age required by local law in your country. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has used the App and provided us with personal data, we will delete it.

3. What data we collect

3.1 Disclaimer acceptance

Before using the App, you are required to read and accept a disclaimer regarding the informational nature of its content. In the current state of the App, this acceptance is recorded locally on your device and is not associated with a verifiable personal identity, as the App does not yet require account creation.

Note: when we introduce account-based registration (see Section 9), disclaimer acceptance will be tied to your user profile, and this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

3.2 Portfolio data

If you use the Portfolio feature to record your positions, investment scenarios, or other personal financial data, this data is stored exclusively locally on your device. We do not transmit it, do not save it on our servers, and have no access to it in any form. If you uninstall the App or switch devices without a backup, this data will be lost.

3.3 Subscription and payment data

If you subscribe to a paid plan, the purchase and payment process is handled directly by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store), through their respective billing systems, potentially supported by the RevenueCat platform for subscription management. We do not receive or store your payment card data. We may receive limited information regarding your subscription status (active, expired, renewed) and an identifier associated with your store account, necessary to grant you access to paid features.

3.4 Technical and usage data

Like most applications, RosCockpit may automatically collect minimal technical information necessary for the service to function (for example, IP address in requests to our servers, device type, operating system version). This data is used solely to ensure the App's proper technical operation and is not used to build advertising profiles.

4. How we use your data

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's features (market analysis, dashboard, technical calculations)
  • Verify the status of your subscription, if any
  • Ensure the technical security of the service
  • Respond to support requests you send us directly

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Where we operate and regulatory compliance

RosCockpit is available in several geographic regions, including Italy, the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Japan, and Australia. Depending on your region of residence, the processing of your data may be subject to specific regulations, including:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) for users in Italy, the European Union, and the United Kingdom
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) for users residing in California, United States
  • Other local regulations applicable in your country of residence

If you are a resident of the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the right to: access your data, request rectification or deletion, object to processing, and request data portability. Since the App does not currently collect identifiable personal data (see Section 3), these rights primarily apply to subscription data managed through Apple/Google/RevenueCat — to exercise them in relation to that data, please contact those services directly, in addition to us.

7. Data security

We adopt reasonable technical measures to protect the information we process. However, no data transmission or storage system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention

Your Portfolio data remains on your device until you delete it yourself or uninstall the App. The minimal technical data that passes through our servers (Section 3.4) is not retained beyond the time necessary for the service to function.

9. Future changes to this Policy

RosCockpit is under active development. In particular, we plan to introduce:

  • User account authentication (for subscription management and data synchronization)
  • Paid subscription system via RevenueCat

When these features become active, we will update this Privacy Policy to accurately reflect the newly collected data (e.g., email address associated with the account) and, where required by applicable law, request your renewed explicit consent. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the processing of your data, write to us at: info@roscockpit.com

This document is an evolving draft, appropriate to RosCockpit's current stage of development (pre-public launch, TestFlight distribution). It will be reviewed and finalized before public release on the App Store and Google Play Store.
